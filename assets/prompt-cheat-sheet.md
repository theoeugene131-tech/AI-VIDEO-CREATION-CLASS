# AI Video Creation Toolkit — Prompt Cheat Sheet

*Bonus resource for "AI Video Creation for Beginners" students.*

---

## The Prompt Formula

Every usable AI video prompt has four parts:

**SUBJECT + ACTION + CAMERA + STYLE**

| Part | What it answers | Example |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| Subject | Who or what is in the frame? | A plate of jollof rice on a wooden table |
| Action | What is it doing? | Being served, steam rising |
| Camera | How does the frame move? | Slow push-in / static / tracking from the side |
| Style | What does it look like? | Warm cinematic light, shallow depth of field |

**Rule:** if you cannot say it in one sentence, it is two shots.

---

## Good vs Bad Prompts

| Bad (random, unusable) | Good (specific, usable) |
|------------------------|--------------------------|
| "Make a cool video of a restaurant" | "Close-up of jollof rice being served, steam rising, slow push-in, warm cinematic light" |
| "A busy day with customers and food and a happy vibe" | "Friends laughing around a table, candid phone-cam style, warm light" |
| "Something cinematic and viral" | "Exterior of a shop in morning light, slow push-in toward the sign, cinematic" |
| "A chef cooking" | "Hands seasoning a pot, close-up, warm cinematic light, shallow depth of field" |

---

## Camera Moves Cheat Sheet

- **Slow push-in** — camera moves slowly toward the subject (builds focus)
- **Static** — locked-off shot, no movement (great for products/titles)
- **Tracking shot** — camera moves alongside the subject
- **Top-down / overhead** — camera looks straight down (food, desks, hands)
- **Whip pan** — fast horizontal move between two similar shots
- **Zoom out** — starts tight, reveals the scene

## Style Words Cheat Sheet

- Warm cinematic light / golden hour
- Cool blue tone / neon night look
- Phone-cam / candid / documentary feel
- Soft studio lighting / clean white background
- 4K / sharp / high contrast
- Shallow depth of field (blurry background)
- Vintage film grain / retro look

## Negative Prompts (what to exclude)

Add these when a tool supports negative prompts:

`no text, no watermark, no extra fingers, no morphing, no flicker, no logo`

---

## The 30-Second Script Template

A 30-second video = 5–6 shots × 3–6 seconds each. One line per shot:

```
Shot 1 (establishing): [subject] + [action] + [camera] + [style]
Shot 2 (hero/detail):  ...
Shot 3 (action):       ...
Shot 4 (atmosphere):   ...
Shot 5 (logo/CTA):     ...
```

## Export Settings Cheat Sheet

| Platform | Resolution | Ratio | FPS |
|----------|-----------|-------|-----|
| TikTok / Reels / Shorts | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | 30 |
| YouTube / Facebook feed | 1920 × 1080 | 16:9 | 30 |
| Facebook / LinkedIn feed | 1080 × 1080 | 1:1 | 30 |

Always export at the highest quality — let the platform compress. Never compress twice.
